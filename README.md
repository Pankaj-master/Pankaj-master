## 🏆 Smart India Hackathon

### Building solutions for real-world problems

**Smart India Hackathon (SIH)** has been an important part of my development journey because it changed how I approach projects.

Instead of starting with:

> *"What technology should I use?"*

I learned to start with:

> **"What problem are we actually trying to solve?"**

SIH brings together research, architecture, development, integration, debugging and presentation — all under real constraints.

```text
REAL PROBLEM
     ↓
UNDERSTAND
     ↓
RESEARCH
     ↓
DESIGN
     ↓
BUILD
     ↓
TEST
     ↓
ITERATE
     ↓
PRESENT
```

### What SIH has taught me

- 🧩 Think about the **problem before the technology**
- 🏗️ Design solutions that can actually be implemented
- 🤝 Collaborate effectively under time constraints
- ⚡ Move quickly from an idea to a working prototype
- 🎯 Think about users, feasibility, scalability and impact

> **SIH is where an idea has to survive contact with reality.**
